It's Halloween night. Veronica Ashcroft and her mania for Halloween parties are
putting new twists on a 110-year-old tradition. It isn't a very nice night for
a party. The rain has been pelting down since early morning, but the weather
hasn't deterred many guests. The ballroom at Ashcroft Farm is filled with oddly
costumed visitors. The rather ridiculous western outfit you are wearing was the
only thing you could find at the costumer's on such short notice, but it's out
of place only for its relative sobriety and taste in this crowd.
